Tag anything with #hashtags

Cover image

You asked for this 61 times, more than anything else on the board. Starting today, typing #work, #travel or any other word with a # in front turns it into a tag.

How it works

  • Tags appear in the sidebar under your journals, with a count next to each.
  • Tap a tag to see every entry that uses it, newest first.
  • Rename a tag once and every entry updates.

Your old entries are tagged too: Driftnote scanned them for hashtags when the update installed, so nothing to do on your side.

What’s next

Dark mode is on the roadmap and coming soon. Keep the suggestions coming.

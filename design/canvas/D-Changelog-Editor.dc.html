Dark mode is here, plus faster search

You asked for it 48 times, so it’s finally here: Driftnote now has a proper dark theme. It follows your phone’s setting, or you can pick one in Settings → Appearance.

What changed

  • Dark theme for the editor, the entry list and the calendar.
  • Search is about 4× faster on journals with more than a thousand entries.
  • Tags now autocomplete as you type #.
